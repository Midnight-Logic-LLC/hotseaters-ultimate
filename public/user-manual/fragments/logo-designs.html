Logo Designs

This page describes the HotSeaters logo system: the approved marks, their intended usage, and the rules that keep the brand consistent across product surfaces, marketing, and partner materials. The canonical logo assets live in the docs/branding/ directory — always pull from there rather than re-exporting from screenshots or slide decks.

For color tokens, typography, and the broader visual system, see the Style Guide.

Mark Variants

HotSeaters ships three logo variants. Pick the one that best fits the surface you are designing for.

Variant Description Typical Use
Combined mark Icon mark plus "HotSeaters" wordmark on a single horizontal lockup. Marketing pages, email footers, login screen, investor decks.
Wordmark "HotSeaters" typographic lockup without the icon. Narrow headers, business documents, invoice templates, PDFs.
Icon mark Standalone symbol only. App icon, favicon, avatar, compact nav, social profile picture.

Design Concepts

The icon explores two thematic directions, both rendered in the HotSeaters signature gradient (deep navy through light blue, purple, red, and bright orange).

Concept Symbolism Notes
Adaptation (chameleon) Ability to adapt to complex, shifting trial workflows and integrate many processes into one tool. Left-facing profile, tail curled beneath body. Gradient runs head (navy) to tail (orange). No branch is drawn.
Community (honeycomb) The HotSeatHub marketplace — independent trial-tech professionals collaborating as a network. Geometric honeycomb pattern with gradient shading across cells.

Abstract "business integration" and "community connection" symbols are also reserved as alternate families; the chameleon and honeycomb are the primary production concepts.

Color Variants

Variant When to Use
Full gradient Primary. White or very light backgrounds.
Solid mono (dark) Single-color print, faxes, stamps, embroidery up to moderate detail.
Solid mono (light / knockout) Dark photographic backgrounds or brand-color fills where the gradient would lose contrast.

Color tokens used in the gradient and in mono variants are defined in the Style Guide.

Clearspace and Minimum Size

Rule Specification
Clearspace Minimum padding around the mark equal to the cap height of the "H" in the wordmark (or the width of one honeycomb cell for the icon-only mark).
Minimum size — combined mark 120 px wide on screen; 1 in wide in print.
Minimum size — wordmark 96 px wide on screen; 0.75 in wide in print.
Minimum size — icon mark 24 px on screen (16 px favicon variant provided separately).

Approved and Unapproved Backgrounds

Background Approved Variant
White / off-white Full gradient (preferred).
Light neutral / light brand tint Full gradient, provided AA contrast holds; otherwise solid mono dark.
Dark solid Knockout mono (light).
Photographic Knockout mono over a darkened overlay; never place the gradient mark directly on a busy photograph.
Brand gradient fill Knockout mono only — never place the gradient mark on the gradient.

Unapproved Treatments

  • Do not recolor the mark outside of the defined variants.
  • Do not apply drop shadows, bevels, outer glows, or outlines.
  • Do not rotate, skew, or stretch the mark.
  • Do not rearrange the wordmark and icon relationship.
  • Do not place the full-gradient mark on backgrounds that break contrast requirements.
  • Do not re-typeset the wordmark — always use the supplied vector asset.
  • Do not enclose the mark in a shape (circle, rounded rectangle, etc.) unless using the approved app-icon lockup.

Asset Files

All production files are stored in the docs/branding/ directory of this repository. Refer to the brand template and brand guide there for the authoritative swatches, typographic pairings, and export presets. Do not duplicate those assets into feature code — import from the branding directory or the corresponding public/brand/ export path.
